Free, for everyone

Dackel is free to download and use. No account needed.

Your notes are yours

Dackel doesn't collect your data: no analytics, no trackers. Your notes are plain Markdown files in folders you choose.

Quick Capture from any app

Press ⌃⌥⌘N wherever you are and jot a thought into a new note or today's daily note, without leaving what you're doing.

Type / for quick formatting

Type / at the start of a line to add a heading, a list or a checklist without reaching for the mouse.

The easiest way to edit tables

Click into a cell and type. Add rows and columns with a click, and the table stays plain Markdown underneath.

See what links here

See which notes link to the one you're reading, and where it links to.

See how your notes connect

A graph of your folder shows how your notes link to each other. Pick a note to open it.

Every version, kept

Dackel saves earlier versions of your notes as you write. Go back to any of them, or undo a single change.

Everything about a note, at a glance

Word count and reading time, properties and tags, and the note's version history, all in one panel beside your writing.

Every command, a keystroke away

Open the command palette and type to find any command, from New Note to Insert Table, with its shortcut beside it.
