Journaling

A page for every day.

One note per day, started from your own template, kept as plain files on your Mac. Nobody else reads your journal, including us.

Today's note, one click away

Open Today's Note and Dackel makes the page for you, named by the date and filled from your daily template, with {{date}} and your prompts already in place.

Write it down the moment it happens

Press ⌃⌥⌘N in any app and your thought lands in today's note, with the time if you like, without leaving what you're doing.

Meetings

Walk out with the action items.

Start every meeting on the same page and leave with a list of everything you promised to do.

Every meeting starts the same way

Name new notes automatically, like Meeting 2026-11-08 1000, and start them from a template with attendees, agenda and next steps.

Every open task, in one list

Press ⌃⌘T to see the unchecked tasks from all your notes, grouped by meeting. Check one off there, or jump straight to where it was written.

Research, science & math

From first idea to finished paper.

Write $E = mc^2$ and see it typeset. Draw a diagram from a few lines of text. It all stays plain Markdown you can open anywhere.

Ready to hand in

Export a PDF with selectable text, typeset math and diagrams, working footnote links and bookmarks for every heading.

Name the drafts that matter

Mark a version “Sent to advisor” and it's kept for good. Later, see exactly what changed since then, and undo any single change.

Linked notes

Notes that know each other.

Link one idea to the next with [[wikilinks]], then see where everything leads. Open the folder of notes you already have and start right away.

See what links here

The Links panel shows every note that mentions this one, with the sentence around each mention.

Rename without breaking anything

Rename or move a note and every link to it is updated, in every note in the folder.

And everything underneath.

  • Command palette

    Find any command by typing, with its shortcut beside it.

  • Type / to format

    Headings, lists, checklists and templates without the mouse.

  • Tables you can click into

    Edit cells and add rows in place. Still plain Markdown.

  • Two notes side by side

    Open a second note in a split pane to read one and write the other.

  • Search that understands notes

    Filter by folder, tag or property, like status=active.

  • Focus mode

    Hide the sidebar and panels, and keep only the page.

  • Everything at a glance

    Word count, reading time, properties and tags beside your writing.

  • Rendered, Hybrid or Source

    See formatted text, the Markdown, or both.

  • Nothing lost

    Autosave, recovery after a crash, and Recently Deleted for notes you want back.

Free, for everyone

Dackel is free to download and use. No account needed.

Your notes are yours

Dackel doesn't collect your data: no analytics, no trackers. Your notes are plain Markdown files in folders you choose.
