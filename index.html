Dackel In development

Markdown.Made for Mac.

A native Markdown editor for your everyday writing.

Plain Markdown files

Your notes live in your own folders as .md files. Open them in Dackel, Finder, or another editor.

Linked notes

Connect your notes with links and see which notes link back. Follow an idea from one note to the next.

Quick Capture

Capture a thought from any app with a keyboard shortcut. Save it to your inbox or today’s note.

Dackel is an independent app, still taking shape.
