Writing

Just you and the page.

Focus mode hides everything but your words. Your draft stays a plain text file you can open anywhere.

Know where you stand

Word count and reading time as you write, and for any passage you select.

Every draft, kept

Compare today's version with last week's, and bring back any sentence you cut.

Journalism

Get it down before it's gone.

A quote, a lead, a phone number: press ⌃⌥⌘N in any app and it's saved.

Sources, one share away

Share a web page to Dackel and it arrives as a link, with the text you selected quoted above it.

A log for every day

Send captures straight to today's note, each one stamped with the time.

Study

From lecture notes to finished paper.

Write math the way you would in LaTeX and see it typeset as you go.

Ready for every lecture

Start each class from your own template, with the course, the date and room for questions already in place.

Ready to hand in

Export a PDF with typeset equations, footnotes and a bookmark for every heading.

Research

See how your sources connect.

Link papers, ideas and findings with [[wikilinks]], and the graph shows where they lead.

Find the paper you need

Search by what you've noted about it, like status=read or a tag, together with the words inside it.

Keep the PDF with the note

Drop a PDF onto a note. Dackel copies it next to the note and adds a link to it.

Engineering

Docs that draw their own diagrams.

Describe a flow in a few lines of Mermaid and Dackel draws it. The diagram lives in the same text file as your docs.

Tables without the pipes

Click into a cell and type, or add rows and columns with a click. Underneath, it's still a Markdown table.

Markdown when you want it

Switch to Source to see every character exactly as it's saved.

Work

Walk out with the action items.

Start every meeting the same way, and leave with a list of everything you agreed to do.

Notes that name themselves

“Meeting 2026-11-09 1000”, with attendees, agenda and next steps ready to fill in.

Every open task, in one list

Press ⌃⌘T to see unchecked tasks from all your notes, grouped by meeting. Tick them off right there.

Free, for everyone

Dackel is free to download and use. No account needed.

Your notes are yours

Dackel doesn't collect your data: no analytics, no trackers. Your notes are plain Markdown files in folders you choose.
