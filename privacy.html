Privacy

This notice covers the Dackel app for macOS and dackelapp.com. Neither uses advertising or analytics trackers.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

The app

Dackel processes your notes on your Mac and saves them in folders you choose. Your notes are not uploaded to us. The app does not require an account or send us usage analytics.

Settings, workspace information, drafts, recovery copies, and note history are stored on your device or alongside your files to support editing and recovery.

Cloud folders

If you keep your notes in iCloud Drive or another synced folder, that provider may upload and sync the files under its own settings and privacy policy. Dackel does not operate a cloud storage service.

The website

This site has no analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, or accounts. Its code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Images are served with the site, and fonts come from your device.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Website delivery and security rely on our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. See GitHub’s Pages documentation and privacy statement for its processing, retention, and international transfers.

Contacting support

If you email support@rblch.com, we receive your address, message, and any attachments. We use them to respond and follow up. Please share only what is needed to explain your enquiry.

For EEA users, this processing relies on steps related to a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where applicable, or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries under Article 6(1)(f).

Retention and deletion

You control your saved notes and local copies. Recovery and history features can retain earlier or deleted content. Removing the app does not automatically delete your note folders or copies held by backup or sync services.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any related obligations or legal claims. You can request deletion by email. We cannot remotely delete notes on your device or copies held by your backup or sync provider.

Publisher and your rights

The publisher responsible for the app and website and their postal details are listed in the Impressum. For privacy questions or requests, email support@rblch.com.

Depending on the circumstances, applicable law gives you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal data, and to receive a portable copy. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it. You can also complain to a data-protection authority.
